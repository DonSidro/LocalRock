# Room selection and map appearance

Tap a room's scanned floor area or its label to add it to the cleaning selection. Tap it
again to remove it. Selected rooms have a deeper fill, a stronger outline and a numbered
label showing cleaning order. Selecting rooms does not start a clean or change the robot's
reported active cleaning targets.

Room colors remain distinct in light and dark themes. When labels crowd together, they
move apart and a short line connects a moved label to its room. Selected labels take
priority. Pinch to zoom for more space; double tap returns to the framed map.

The Layers menu controls room icons, cleaning path, restrictions and detected obstacles.
Map appearance does not modify the robot's room boundaries, restrictions or saved map.

During an active cleaning task, room emphasis follows the targets reported by the
robot. Room selection becomes available again when the task finishes.
